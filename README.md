# POS System — Multi-Business SaaS Platform

A modern, scalable Point of Sale (POS) and business management platform designed to help businesses streamline sales, track inventory, manage customers, and monitor their operations through a centralized digital system.

## Overview

This project aims to deliver a commercial, multi-tenant SaaS solution that can serve multiple businesses across different industries. Rather than being limited to a single type of business, the system is being designed to support retail shops, hardware stores, timber and building materials suppliers, furniture businesses, and other small and medium-sized enterprises.

The long-term vision is to provide business owners with an affordable, reliable, and easy-to-use platform for managing daily operations while enabling a platform administrator to oversee the wider SaaS ecosystem.

## Planned Features

- **Point of Sale:** Process sales and manage transactions efficiently.
- **Inventory Management:** Track products, stock levels, and inventory movements.
- **Customer Management:** Maintain customer records and purchase histories.
- **Billing and Receipts:** Generate transaction records, bills, and sales receipts.
- **Business Management:** Organize business information and operational data.
- **Sales Reporting:** Monitor sales performance and business activity.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Support multiple businesses with logically isolated data.
- **Super Admin Dashboard:** Provide centralized platform administration and business account oversight.
- **Subscription Management:** Support future SaaS pricing plans and subscription packages.
- **Scalable Database:** Use PostgreSQL for reliable relational data management.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Vite
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express, TypeScript
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Database Tooling:** Prisma
- **Version Control:** Git and GitHub

## Project Vision

The goal is to build a production-ready POS SaaS platform that businesses can access through subscription plans, with the potential for additional deployment options as the platform matures.

The architecture is intended to remain industry-agnostic, allowing new business types and features to be introduced without rebuilding the entire system.

## Development Status

Currently under active development. Features listed above represent the product roadmap and are not necessarily implemented yet.
